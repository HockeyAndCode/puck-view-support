# Getting started and troubleshooting

Your iPhone shows where to go; your Apple Watch recognizes your wrist movement at each spot. Puck View doesn't track the puck or count stick touches.

## Set up

1. Wear the Watch on your **bottom hand** on the stick. Top-hand use isn't tested yet.
2. Stand the iPhone in landscape where you can see it with your head up, with room to reach all around you.
3. Open Puck View on both devices and allow motion and workout access if asked.

Keep the same grip and stance. Left and right always mean your physical left and right, whichever way you shoot.

## Players

Tap your name at the top of Home to switch players or add one. Each player keeps their own calibrations, Watch setup, best scores and history on this iPhone. Settings are shared by everyone.

## Calibrate every Watch session

The Watch forgets which way you face each time its session restarts, so calibrate once per session:

- **Full calibration** (first time, or a new setup): a short grip countdown, then about 7 seconds at each of the five spots: Front left, Front center, Front right, Rear left and Rear right. Rear spots are behind your body on each side. Follow the countdown and the Watch's taps, and stickhandle normally at each spot.
- **Quick calibration** (after that): stickhandle at **Front center** only. Your first drill checks it lines up; if no target is reached in 20 seconds, Puck View suggests calibrating again.

## Train

- **Timed Trial** (free): 30 seconds, a random spot each time. Stickhandle at the lit spot; when your Watch confirms you're there it turns green and the next one lights up. Your score is the spots you reach. After each set you see your best, your average time to reach a spot, your fastest and slowest spot, and the drill that trains your slowest one.
- **Drill library:** continuous drills by level. **Level 1** is free; **Levels 2 and 3** unlock with one in-app purchase, which you can restore on any device with the same Apple Account. Keep the puck moving along the drawn route; the next target lights up as you reach each one. Each drill has sets with a rest between, which you can skip.
- **Progress:** your best Timed Trial of each recent day, and your time to each spot.

Times are how long it takes from a spot lighting up until your Watch confirms you're there. They aren't puck speed or a technique score.

## Settings

- **Rest between sets:** for library drills, and before a Timed Trial when you choose to rest.
- **Watch vibrations:** the taps on the 3-2-1 and the buzzes at each target and set end.
- **Your data:** what's stored, the privacy policy, **Delete recordings** and **Delete all data**.

## Messages

- **"Waiting for Watch data":** keep stickhandling at the spot, keep the Watch near the phone and both apps open. The drill waits for fresh data.
- **A spot needs repeating during calibration:** two spots looked too alike, or a recording moved too much. Use your normal grip and stance, keep dribbling at the spot for the whole recording, and repeat the ones named. Don't hold still or exaggerate your wrist angle.
- **"Unlock your Watch" or the Watch doesn't answer:** unlock the Watch, open Puck View on it, and tap **Retry**.
- **"Calibrate again":** the Watch session restarted. Run Quick calibration or a full calibration.

A brief dropout never needs deleting the app or unpairing the Watch.

## Your recordings

Recordings stay on the iPhone. **Watch calibration → Recordings → Export recordings** shares a copy only when you choose, without player names. To delete, use **Settings → Your data** in the app. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

## Install through TestFlight

Install **TestFlight** on your paired iPhone and accept your invitation. Install Puck View on the phone, then open its TestFlight **App Details** and install the Watch app from the Apple Watch section. Open both apps after updating. No Mac is needed. See [Apple's TestFlight instructions](https://testflight.apple.com/).
